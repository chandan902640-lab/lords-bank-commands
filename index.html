Tips

  • If a player's name has a space you must either: Use an underscore within the command (Example: Player 1 becomes Player_1 → !setacc Player_1), OR use quotes around the name (Example: "Player 1" → !setacc "Player 1").
  • By default Authorized users balances are non-deductible (Unlimited balance); an Authorized user is essentially an administrator of the bank. They cannot use the Donate command.
  • By default R4+ have similar access to Authorized users. Please ensure your command minimum ranks are appropriate.
  • The bank will not respond during long running tasks, such as hero stages. We suggest in your hero stage to: Set a custom chapter and stage with 3 unlocked and enable Sweep and X10 options.

Search Commands

Note: Append chat at the end to post coordinates into guild chat (e.g. !findtile food 4 chat). Radius is ~70 tiles.

Command Function Example Result
!findtile [type] [level] Finds resource tiles around castle bank !findtile food 4 Bank searches tile level 4 and sends mail
!findtile any [level] Finds any type of tile around castle bank !findtile any 4 Searches any tile lvl 4 and sends mail
!findtilelocal [type] [level] Finds tiles around castle who sent command !findtilelocal food 4 Searches tile around sender and sends mail
!findmonster [monster] [level] Finds monster around castle bank !findmonster hardrox 2 Searches hardrox lvl 2 and sends mail
!findmonster any [level] Finds any monster around castle bank !findmonster any 2 Searches any monster lvl 2 and sends mail
!findmonsterlocal [name] [level] Finds monster around castle who sent command !findmonsterlocal hardrox 2 Searches monster around sender and sends mail
!findnest [level] Finds darknest around the bank !findnest 3 Searches darknest lvl 3 around bank and sends mail
!findnestlocal [level] Finds darknest around the castle who sent command !findnestlocal 3 Searches darknest around sender and sends mail

Balance Commands

Command Function Example Result
!bal Checks your balance !bal Report of how much RSS you sent to Bank
!adminbal [player] Check balance of the specific player !bal Shark Report of how much RSS Shark sent to Bank
!adminbal Check the RSS balance of the Bank !adminbal Report of total RSS Bank holds
!adminbag Checks RSS balance of the Bank's Bag !adminbag Report of RSS Bank has in bags
!setbal [player] [type] [amount] Manually sets RSS balance to an account !setbal Shark food 100M Sets Food Balance of Shark to 100M
!setacc [player] Sets all balance sent to be credited to another account !setacc Shark Balance sent will credit to Shark
!transfer [player] [type] [amount] Transfers the Balance to another player !transfer Shark 50M gold 50M gold credited to Shark
!setrsslimit [type] [amount] Sets limit for RSS which bank won't go below !setrsslimit gold 100M If bank has < 100M Gold, won't send gold

Resource Commands

Command Function Example Result
![type] [amount] Sends one specific RSS (food, stone, ore, wood, gold) !food 5M The bank sends 5M Food
!rss [F] [S] [W] [O] [G] Sends all types of RSS (Food, Stone, Wood, Ore, Gold) !rss 5M 5M 5M 5M 0 Bank sends 5M of all RSS (except Gold)
!donate[type] [player] [amount] Sends RSS from your balance to a specific player !donatefood Shark 5M Bank sends 5M Food to "Shark"
!admin[type] [player] [amount] Admin sends specific RSS to a player !adminfood Shark 5M Bank sends 5M Food to "Shark"
!adminrss [F] [S] [W] [O] [G] [player] Admin sends all types of RSS to target player !adminrss 0 5M 5M 5M 5M Shark Sends 5M of RSS (except Food) to "Shark"
